## What

A health assessment tool specifically designed for programmers and developers. Answer 10 questions about your coding lifestyle and get:

- 🩺 **Your "body age"** vs your actual age — the gap might shock you
- ⚠️ **Risk level** — from "Healthy" to "Critical, see a doctor now"
- 📊 **Per-question breakdown** — see exactly which habits are hurting you
- 🎴 **Shareable card** (1080×1440) — compare with your teammates

## Why

From CoolApk hot list: *"I'm only 18 and already diagnosed with pre-diabetes."*
From Weibo #4: *"Hands-up sleeping position is your body crying for help."*

Programmers sit 10+ hours daily, code past midnight, live on coffee and takeout. This tool makes the invisible damage visible.

## The 10 Checks

| Check | What it measures |
|-------|-----------------|
| Sitting duration | Hours per day including commute |
| Late-night coding | How often you code past midnight |
| Exercise frequency | Sessions of 30+ minutes per week |
| Caffeine intake | Coffee / energy drinks per day |
| Meal regularity | Home-cooked vs takeout vs skipped |
| Max screen time | Longest continuous screen session |
| Health checkup | When was your last physical exam |
| Energy levels | Days per week feeling refreshed |
| Musculoskeletal | Neck / back / wrist pain |
| Mental health | Mood stability and stress levels |

## Privacy

100% local. No data collected, no API calls, no tracking. Your health data stays on your device.

## 中文

**你的身体，还撑得住几年的996？**

10道题，60秒，测出你的程序员身体年龄。不是普通的健康测试——每道题都是针对程序员特有的生活方式设计的：久坐时长、熬夜频率、咖啡因摄入、外卖比例、屏幕时间、颈椎/腰椎/手腕状况。

测完之后你会得到：
- 你的"身体年龄"（可能比实际年龄大很多岁）
- 健康风险等级（安全 / 开始透支 / 明显透支 / 严重预警）
- 每道题的详细分析
- 可保存的分享卡片

**所有数据仅在本机计算，不上传任何信息。**
